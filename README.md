# Mycelis website

A lightweight static site hosted on GitHub Pages. Mycelis is the trading brand behind the Mycelis mushroom appliance and Virelis microgreens appliance.

## Pages

- index.html: product-family introduction, prototype progress and the mailing list.
- mycelis.html and virelis.html: product information, Classic/Pro comparisons, intended refills, care and evidence limits.
- about.html and contact.html: business context, development approach and contact.
- privacy.html: the current source-reviewed data flows and remaining privacy checks.
- preorder.html: legacy no-index entry to the separate password-protected sandbox shop, not a live preorder page.

## Preview and hosting

Open index.html or serve this directory using a static HTTP server. Paths are relative; the public pages need no build process or environment variables. Publish this directory at the repository root using the existing main-branch GitHub Pages setup.

The protected shop is separately hosted. Its authentication, catalogue and source do not belong in this public directory. The public Shop links say Coming soon and explain that the destination is a protected sandbox. No real orders, payments, reservations, subscriptions or trial bookings are enabled.

## Content boundaries

- Appliance prices remain in the protected preview, not the public pages or this readme. Public refill prices are indicative and exclude shipping.
- Classic is the tactile-control model; Pro adds a screen, scheduling and estimates. Both retain the same intended growing purpose. Do not infer better Pro harvests or completed Classic automation from renders.
- Keep development observations, intended functions and unvalidated claims distinct. No formal product-safety approval is claimed; customer trials await electrical safety assessment.
- Official refills are optional. Keep DIY support separate from an open-source commitment and preserve consumer rights.
- Refills, packaging, storage, care, warranties and commercial terms remain subject to their stated development limits. Future subscriptions have no fixed schedule or confirmed discount.
- No supplier names, private costs, margins, production targets, R&D figures or proprietary manufacturing details belong in published content.
- Nutrition research is supporting context, not measurements of our harvests or proof the machines improve health, yield, efficiency or cost.

## Assets and checks

Phone layouts use a dedicated breakpoint up to 620px: product and About images come before supporting notes, refill prices use compact rows, comparison columns give more space to model values, and menus/footers retain usable touch targets. The paired homepage hero remains side by side. Styling uses a versioned URL across all pages to avoid mixing old cached CSS with the revised markup.

Virelis has separate crop-research cards for Micro Pak Choy, Cress moss and Kohlrabi purple, with primary-paper links and visible limits. Keep reported study nutrients separate from a product nutrition label; no measured amounts, daily-intake values, high/source descriptors or health benefits are asserted for our harvests. Final seed-lot identities and blend contents still need confirmation.

Retain the existing render/prototype labels and matched photographic frames. Asset filenames and technical model IDs may retain legacy base naming for compatibility; customer-facing labels use Classic. The original images are not altered by the copy update.

Check all seven pages at mobile, tablet and desktop widths, native FAQ/menu keyboard operation, image loading and local links. Check the separate shop's authentication, model/pack selectors and sandbox-only behaviour before deployment. Website checks are not hardware, safety, nutrition or legal validation.

The EmailOctopus embed retains email plus an optional Signup interest field. Signup confirmation/welcome settings and full third-party data flows still need checking. Do not submit test subscribers or enable real payments as part of a copy check.
