The concept

A small indoor growing rhythm.

Verdelis is intended for people who would like to grow microgreens at home in a simple, considered format. It belongs alongside Mycelis, sharing the same product-minded approach while focusing on a different kind of harvest.

Simple by design

A clear growing workflow.

The exact format is still being developed. The intended experience is centred on a straightforward progression from planting through to harvest.
