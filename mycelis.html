How Mycelis works

Three simple steps.

Built for a kitchen bench, without a grow tent or app.

  1. 01

    Insert.

    Place a ready-to-grow oyster mushroom cartridge inside and fill the reservoir.

  2. 02

    Grow.

    Light, airflow, humidity support and on-device guidance help the grow along.

  3. 03

    Harvest.

    Pick the mushrooms, remove the cartridge and start again when ready.
