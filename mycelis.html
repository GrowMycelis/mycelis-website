Mycelis

Grow gourmet mushrooms at home.

Mycelis is a countertop growing system for fresh oyster mushrooms, designed around ready-to-grow cartridges, guided growing, and beautiful harvests from your kitchen bench.

How it works

From cartridge to harvest.

Mycelis is being designed to make gourmet mushroom growing feel simple, clean, and at home on your kitchen bench.

01

Insert

Place a ready-to-grow cartridge inside Mycelis and fill the water reservoir.

02

Grow

The chamber helps maintain the right growing environment while mushrooms develop day by day.

03

Harvest

Pick fresh gourmet mushrooms from your kitchen bench when they are ready.

Designed for modern homes

A growing system designed to fit in.

Mycelis is being developed for people who want the experience of growing mushrooms without grow tents, messy substrate preparation, or complicated equipment.

The system brings together a compact countertop enclosure, ready-to-grow cartridges, and simple on-device guidance.

Ready-to-grow cartridges
No sterilising, mixing substrate, or specialist setup.
Countertop friendly
Designed to look at home in a kitchen, not hidden in a garage.
Guided growing
Simple on-device controls help you follow each grow.
Fresh harvests
Grow beautiful gourmet mushrooms where you can actually see them.

Development status

Currently in development.

Mycelis is being tested across enclosure design, cartridge format, humidity control, airflow, and oyster mushroom varieties.

  1. 01

    Prototype enclosure testing

    Refining the chamber shape, airflow, lighting, water reservoir, and cartridge access.

  2. 02

    Cartridge development

    Testing simple ready-to-grow oyster mushroom cartridges for clean home use.

  3. 03

    Growing condition trials

    Comparing humidity, airflow, light, and timing across oyster mushroom varieties.
