Before you choose

A few things still to settle.

What's different between Base and Pro?

For both Mycelis and Virelis, Base is automatic but screenless, using tactile buttons. It doesn't include growth-duration prediction or automatic light cycles. Pro adds a screen, estimated growth duration and automatic light cycles. Those predictions are estimates, not guaranteed harvest dates. Remaining specifications still need validation, and neither model is available to buy yet.

Will I need a refill subscription?

No subscription will be required. Official refills and compatible DIY consumables are planned for both machines. The Virelis pack prices and optional mat subscription are preliminary plans, not live purchases or billing services.

What happens when I press Request?

Your email app opens a message naming the machine, model and preview price. Review it and send it to us if you're interested. Nothing is sent just by clicking the button, and an enquiry doesn't reserve stock or create an order.

If you don't have an email app set up, write to hello@mycelis.co.nz with your preferred machine and model.

What will I pay, and when will it arrive?

The preview machine prices are NZ$249 for Base and NZ$349 for Pro, for either grower. Before paid preorders open, we'll confirm what's included, shipping, any applicable taxes, the total payable, delivery expectations and cancellation or refund terms. No delivery date is confirmed yet.

Does an enquiry sign me up for marketing emails?

No. We'll use your message to respond to the enquiry. The mailing list is a separate choice. See our privacy information.
