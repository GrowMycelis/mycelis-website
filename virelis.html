Controls and harvest estimates

Classic has three independent light controls, one per tier. Pro adds touchscreen controls, automatic light scheduling and species-based harvest estimates.

Pro estimates use the selected species as a guide to timing, rather than a guaranteed harvest date.

Does Virelis use environmental sensors?

Virelis is sensorless. Automatic water delivery is intended for both models, but there is no environmental sensing, humidity control or fan control. Neither model has active heating or cooling. Pro estimates are species-based, not a live assessment of the crop.

Growing without Wi-Fi

No app or cloud connection is required for growing. Pro can optionally use Wi-Fi to set the date and time. Controls stay on the appliance.

From the bench to your plate

More than a finishing touch.

Colour, variety and something fresh for lunch. Published crop studies have also measured vitamins, minerals and other plant compounds in these microgreens. Here's what that research tells us.

Our intended launch choices: Mixed Greens, Micro Pak Choy, Cress moss and Kohlrabi purple.

What researchers measured in the crops. These are not nutrition labels for our refills.

Micro Pak Choy.

  • Vitamin C
  • Lutein
  • Beta-carotene

Pak choi microgreen research measured vitamin C, lutein and beta-carotene, a compound the body can use to make vitamin A. This is crop research, not a tested Virelis harvest. [1]

Cress moss.

  • Vitamin C
  • Plant polyphenols

Moss-curled cress is garden cress, Lepidium sativum. Published research measured vitamin C and phenolic compounds in its microgreens. Add its peppery leaves to sandwiches or eggs; our own harvests have not been nutritionally tested. [2]

Kohlrabi purple.

  • Vitamin C
  • Calcium
  • Potassium

Researchers measured vitamin C, calcium, potassium, lutein and beta-carotene in Purple Vienna kohlrabi microgreens. The colour is only part of the story; amounts vary with the crop and growing conditions. [1]

We haven't lab-tested a Virelis harvest. These are findings from published crop research, not guaranteed nutrient amounts, daily-intake percentages or proof of health benefits from our refills or appliance. Variety, growing conditions, harvest stage, storage and portion size matter. Final seed-lot identities and blend contents still need confirmation.

Mixed Greens is a blend of several seed varieties on each mat, not an assortment of separate mats. It is the only blend grown in Virelis so far; the other choices are being sourced. We haven't measured comparisons with normal growing kits or supermarket produce, and make no efficiency, yield, cost-saving or nutritional-superiority claim.

The growing routine

Start with a mat. Finish with lunch.

The intended setup needs Virelis, pre-seeded mats and water. Mats germinate directly inside the machine without blackout covers. Use up to three mats, one per tier; unused tiers can remain empty.

Each tier has its own pump, fed from a shared reservoir. The tiers operate independently, so different varieties can be grown at different stages. Water does not recirculate. The current prototype still needs manual control of some functions; spray coverage, drip control and the final care routine need more testing.

You'll check the water level visually and top up as needed. Harvest by cutting the greens with scissors: one harvest per mat, then a fresh mat for the next grow. Refilling, draining and cleaning guidance will be confirmed after testing.

Leave room in front to get the trays in and out, and space behind for ventilation. Required clearances still need testing. We'll publish measured external dimensions and weight before launch; the mat size below is not an appliance dimension.

About using Virelis

A few practical questions

Can I grow different varieties on each tier?

Yes. The three tiers have independent water delivery, so you can grow different varieties or start each tier on a different day. Use one mat per tier, and leave any unused tiers empty.

What size are the seed mats?

Virelis seed mats measure 12 × 12 cm (120 × 120 mm), with one mat per tier. Each mat is single-use and provides one harvest.

Can I mix varieties in a pack?

The planned range includes Mixed Greens, Micro Pak Choy, Cress moss and Kohlrabi purple. Choose varieties in groups of three mats: a 9-pack could contain 3 + 3 + 3, 6 + 3, or nine of one variety. Mixed Greens is a seed blend on each mat.

Can I choose one-off refills or make my own?

Yes. Our planned 9-, 18- and 27-mat packs are one-off refills, with no subscription required. Compatible DIY mats are welcome too. We plan to share fit information and basic DIY guidance at launch.

Which controls can I choose?

Classic has three independent light buttons, one per tier. Pro adds a touchscreen, automatic light scheduling and species-based harvest estimates. Growing does not require an app or cloud connection. See the model comparison for planned features.
