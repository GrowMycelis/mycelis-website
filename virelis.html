Virelis

A countertop microgreens grower

A few trays of greens, close to the kitchen.

Virelis is a compact, three-tier countertop microgreens grower, designed to automate watering and lighting. We're developing pre-seeded mats that you can insert into the machine, with support for compatible homemade mats planned too.

We've built the first physical prototype. The growing system, refills and final model features are still being tested; nothing is on sale yet.

Why microgreens?

Little leaves. More ways to use them.

Snip some over eggs, tuck them into a sandwich or add a handful to a salad. Studies have measured vitamins C, E and K1, plus beta-carotene, in microgreens. The amounts vary a lot by variety. [1]

A few examples to explore — not a confirmed Virelis seed range.

Radish.

Peppery leaves for a sandwich or salad. In a study of 25 microgreens, green daikon radish had the highest vitamin E concentration among the samples tested. [1]

Pea shoots.

A sweeter-tasting option to tuck into a wrap. Researchers have also measured vitamin C in pea microgreens grown under controlled conditions. [2]

Red cabbage.

A splash of purple in a bowl of greens. Red cabbage led the vitamin C measurements in that same 25-variety study. Different leaves bring different things to the table. [1]

We haven't lab-tested a Virelis harvest. Seed variety, light, growing conditions, harvest stage, storage and portion size can change what you eat. These findings aren't a promise of health effects or higher nutrition from the machine, and a small garnish doesn't replace a varied diet.

Sources & what they tell us
  1. Xiao and colleagues, 2012 — 25 commercially grown microgreens. Measurements of vitamins and carotenoids, with substantial differences between varieties. This was a food-composition study, not a trial of health outcomes or Virelis.
  2. Balik and colleagues, 2025 — nutritional profiles of six microgreens. Included pea microgreens grown under controlled conditions. The vitamin C result describes those samples, not a guaranteed amount in a home grow.

The planned growing routine

Start with a mat. Finish with lunch.

Our pre-seeded mats are being developed to take the sowing and measuring out of starting a grow. The plan is one mat on each tier, with the machine handling watering and lighting.

You'll still check the grow, top up water as directed, harvest the greens and clean between cycles. The automatic system and care routine are still being validated.

Leave room in front to get the trays in and out. We'll publish the finished appliance dimensions before launch; the proposed mat size below is not the machine's size.

About using Virelis

What you might want to know

Can I make my own Virelis seed mats?

That's part of the plan. We're designing for compatible homemade mats as well as our own pre-seeded refills. The proposed size is 125 × 125 mm; thickness, suitable materials and seed guidance still need validation. It isn't universal compatibility with any mat.

Do I need a subscription?

No subscription is planned as a requirement. One-off refill packs and compatible DIY mats are intended alternatives. A future subscription would be an optional convenience, with skipping, pausing and cancellation; it isn't available yet.

How many growing cycles does a nine-mat pack provide?

Three complete growing cycles when you use all three tiers: three mats per cycle, one on each tier. This doesn't promise a particular harvest size, regrowth or time to harvest.

How long do seed mats last in storage?

Shelf life is still being tested. We'll publish storage conditions and a validated storage period before sale, rather than guess how long a pack will last.

Will refill packs be available individually?

Yes, one-off packs are planned alongside the optional subscription. The preliminary pack options aren't available to buy yet.

Can I choose different microgreen varieties?

We're exploring packs with different varieties so you can grow a mixture of crops. The final seed range, pack mixes and whether you can choose each variety are still to be confirmed.

Will every model have a screen?

The prototype pictured here has a screen with separate growing levels, a day count and light switches. That doesn't mean every production model will have one. Base and Pro features and controls are still being finalised.

How will watering and lighting work?

Virelis is being designed to automate both. The water system, lighting routines and refill guidance are still being tested, so we haven't published final settings or maintenance instructions yet.

Does growing indoors make microgreens automatically safe to eat?

No. Seeds, water, growing medium and handling can all introduce contamination. Use seeds sold for edible microgreens and clean drinking water, keep hands and harvesting tools clean, and clean trays between grows. Refrigerate harvested greens. These are basic precautions, not a safety guarantee; we'll publish product-specific care instructions before launch. Microgreen food-safety guidance (PDF).

Can I order one now?

Not yet. We have a physical prototype, but no confirmed price or release date. The mailing list is where we'll share the next build and announce those details when they're settled.
