Virelis

A countertop microgreens grower

A few trays of greens, close to the kitchen.

Virelis stacks three growing shelves in one unit, with lighting and a touchscreen. Sow a tray, keep an eye on it, and cut the greens when they're ready.

We've built the first physical prototype. The growing setup is still being worked through.

Using the shelves

Keep the trays together, and within reach.

The shelves put several trays in one place without spreading them across the bench. The open front gives you access to each level to check the grow or take a tray out.

You'll still sow the trays, look after the growing medium and harvest the greens. We're building the lights and controls into the unit to help with that routine.

Leave room in front to get the trays in and out, and make sure you can reach the top shelf comfortably. We'll publish the finished dimensions before asking anyone to buy one.

About using Virelis

What you might want to know

What can I grow in it?

Virelis is being built for trays of microgreens. We're still working through the tray setup and seed options, so there isn't a confirmed starter range to choose from yet.

What does the screen control?

The current screen shows the growing levels separately, with a day count and a light switch for each one. We're still refining the interface as we use the prototype.

How will watering work?

We'll confirm the watering setup alongside the finished tray design and care instructions. The render alone shouldn't be taken as a promise of automatic watering.

Can I order one now?

Not yet. We have a physical prototype, but no confirmed price or release date. The mailing list is where we'll share the next build and announce those details when they're settled.
