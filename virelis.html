Fresh microgreens, a tray at a time.

A compact, three-tier countertop appliance designed to automate watering and help grow fresh microgreens indoors.

How it's designed to work

Start with a mat. Finish with lunch.

Put a pre-seeded mat in a tray, add water to the shared reservoir and start the level you want to use. The design gives each tier its own pump, so you can start one tray today and another later.

Leave unused tiers empty. Check the water as the greens grow, then snip them with scissors. Each mat is used for one harvest.

Controls and harvest estimates

Classic has three independent light controls, one per tier. Pro adds touchscreen controls, automatic light scheduling and species-based harvest estimates.

Pro estimates use the selected species as a guide to timing, rather than a guaranteed harvest date.

Does Virelis use environmental sensors?

Virelis is sensorless. Automatic water delivery is intended for both models, but there is no environmental sensing, humidity control or fan control. Neither model has active heating or cooling. Pro estimates are species-based, not a live assessment of the crop.

From the bench to your plate

More than a finishing touch.

Colour, variety and something fresh for lunch. Published crop studies have also measured vitamins, minerals and other plant compounds in these microgreens. Here's what that research tells us.

Our intended launch choices: Mixed Greens, Micro Pak Choy, Cress moss and Kohlrabi purple.

Micro Pak Choy.

  • Vitamin C
  • Lutein
  • Beta-carotene

Pak choi microgreen research measured vitamin C, lutein and beta-carotene, a compound the body can use to make vitamin A. This is crop research, not a tested Virelis harvest. [1]

Cress moss.

  • Vitamin C
  • Plant polyphenols

Moss-curled cress is garden cress, Lepidium sativum. Published research measured vitamin C and phenolic compounds in its microgreens. Add its peppery leaves to sandwiches or eggs; our own harvests have not been nutritionally tested. [2]

Kohlrabi purple.

  • Vitamin C
  • Calcium
  • Potassium

Researchers measured vitamin C, calcium, potassium, lutein and beta-carotene in Purple Vienna kohlrabi microgreens. The colour is only part of the story; amounts vary with the crop and growing conditions. [1]

These studies measured the crops, not Virelis harvests. They don't establish nutrient amounts or health benefits for our refills. Exact seed-lot identities and blend contents still need confirmation.

Sources & what they tell us
  1. Kyriacou and colleagues, 2020: pak choi and purple kohlrabi. Tables 3–4 report minerals, vitamin C and carotenoids in Red Wizard F1 pak choi and Purple Vienna kohlrabi under controlled conditions. These are not our seed lots or growing system. Funded by the Italian Space Agency; authors declared no conflicts.
  2. Šola and colleagues, 2024: garden-cress microgreens. Table 2 reports vitamin C and phenolic compounds in Lepidium sativum. Laboratory digestion and extract results do not establish human health benefits. Funded by the Croatian Science Foundation; authors declared no conflicts.
  3. Xiao and colleagues, 2012: 25 commercially grown microgreens. Measured vitamins and carotenoids varied substantially among the research samples. Those findings do not identify our unconfirmed seed blends or establish health outcomes from Virelis.
  4. Balik and colleagues, 2025: nutritional profiles of six microgreens. Further background on variation between research crops, not measurements of our harvests or a specification for Mixed Greens.

A few practical questions

Can I start each tray on a different day?

That's the design: each tier has its own pump, so different trays can be at different stages. Use one mat per tier and leave unused tiers empty.

What size are the seed mats?

12 × 12 cm (120 × 120 mm), with one mat per tier. Each mat is intended for one harvest.

Can I grow without Wi-Fi?

Yes. No app or cloud connection is required for growing. Wi-Fi on Pro is optional for setting the date and time.
