About Mycelis

Kitchen appliances, with a growing side.

We design growing appliances in Christchurch, New Zealand, with a simple idea: fresh produce deserves a place in the everyday kitchen.

Mycelis brings oyster mushrooms to the bench. Virelis makes room for microgreens. Both bring the growing space and controls together in a considered, distinctive design.

Designed in Christchurch

Something you'd leave on the bench.

We care about how an appliance fits into a home: its proportions, finish, controls and the small details you use every day. The growing system and the object around it are designed together.

Design and most manufacturing happen in-house in Christchurch, with selected overseas components. Keeping that work close lets us refine the details as a whole.

Growing your way

Designed around everyday routines.

Keep it on the appliance.

Classic keeps the controls tactile. Pro adds a touchscreen and light scheduling. Both are designed to grow without an app or cloud connection.

A choice of refills.

Choose the convenience of ready-to-grow cartridges and pre-seeded mats, or make compatible refills yourself. Official refills are optional, and no subscription is required.
