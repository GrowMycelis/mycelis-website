About Mycelis + Verdelis

Growing, made more at home.

Mycelis and Verdelis are being developed as two related growing systems for everyday homes.

The project

A product family with one point of view.

Mycelis is focused on fresh oyster mushrooms. Verdelis is a related concept for growing microgreens indoors. While each product has its own purpose, both are being designed around the same belief: growing something fresh should feel approachable, considered, and at home in the kitchen.

The work is being developed in Christchurch, New Zealand, with the product experience kept deliberately clear and practical as the concepts take shape.

The approach

Simple, clear, considered.

01

Built around the home

Products intended to belong in everyday living spaces, not be hidden away.

02

Made approachable

Growing experiences shaped to feel clear for people without specialist knowledge.

Developed with care

Both products remain in development as the details of each growing experience are refined.
