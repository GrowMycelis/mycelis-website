About Mycelis and Virelis

We're building these in Christchurch.

Mycelis is for oyster mushrooms. Virelis is for microgreens. The aim is to make growing a little food at home easier to fit into an ordinary kitchen.

Both are now physical prototypes. This site is a place to show you the machines, explain how they're meant to work, and share the progress.

What we're trying to get right

It has to work on a kitchen bench.

There's more to that than making a nice box. Can you get a cartridge in and out easily? Can you reach the lower tray? Is it obvious what the screen is telling you?

Those are the everyday details we're paying attention to alongside the lights, controls and growing environment. A machine that gets used needs to be easy to live with.
