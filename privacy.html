Joining the list

We use EmailOctopus for the mailing list. When you sign up, your email address and anything else you enter in the signup form are sent to that service. We use the list to send updates about Mycelis and Virelis.

EmailOctopus explains how it handles information in its privacy policy. Every mailing includes a link to unsubscribe.

Hosting and email

The services that host the website and handle email may process routine technical details or the messages you send, under their own policies.

If you have a question about information you've shared with us, email hello@mycelis.co.nz.
