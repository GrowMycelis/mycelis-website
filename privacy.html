Privacy

Your information and the website.

Last updated 17 November 2026.

Joining the list

We use EmailOctopus for the mailing list. When you sign up, your email address and optional Signup interest choice are sent to that service. We use the list to send updates about Mycelis and Virelis, including development news and announcements when orders or preorders open. Your optional choice helps us understand whether you're following development or interested in a future purchase; it does not reserve a machine or place an order.

Both choices use the same mailing list; purchase-interest subscribers may also receive general development news and preorder announcements. We email when there is meaningful news, not on a fixed schedule. Confirmation and welcome-email settings have not been verified; an immediate welcome message is not promised. EmailOctopus explains how it handles information in its privacy policy. Every email includes a link to unsubscribe.

Product and refill enquiries

The product-interest links open your email app with a suggested subject. Nothing is sent until you send the message. We use the email address, topic and other information you choose to share to respond to your enquiry.

These messages are separate from the EmailOctopus waitlist. Asking about Mycelis, Virelis, DIY compatibility or a future subscription doesn't enrol you in marketing, place an order or authorise payments.

Who runs the site

Mycelis is a trading brand of a Christchurch-based sole-trader business. For support or privacy enquiries, contact hello@mycelis.co.nz. Mycelis currently operates as a sole-trader trading brand, not a limited company.

Hosting and other services

GitHub Pages hosts the public site. Cloudflare hosts the protected shop and its temporary test records. EmailOctopus handles mailing-list submissions; Stripe hosts the sandbox payment form. They have different roles and may process different technical or customer information under their own policies. This is not a claim that all information is shared with every provider.

Google Search Console is used for the site. No additional analytics or advertising pixels were found in the site's own source reviewed on 17 November 2026. This does not complete a review of third-party embed behaviour, provider logs, international processing or account settings.

ChatGPT is used during development. The website itself has no AI chat or direct OpenAI API integration. This is not a statement that no information could ever be used in development tools; customer-data handling still needs review before live sales.

The password-protected sandbox shop

The Shop link opens a private preview hosted on Cloudflare. Checkout uses Stripe sandbox, not live payments. Use fictional contact and address details and Stripe test cards only. It does not place a real order or sign you up for marketing.

Starting checkout saves your selected products and an opaque test reference in this browser tab for up to 24 hours, so cancellation can restore your bag. We store test selections, totals and payment/event references in Cloudflare D1 for up to 30 days. Our shop does not store card details, emails or addresses; details entered in Stripe's hosted test form are handled by Stripe, whose separate sandbox records are not deleted by our shop's cleanup.

Live-order retention and privacy arrangements will be decided before real payments are enabled; the 30-day sandbox cleanup is not a retention promise for every provider or a future live shop.

Closing the tab discards its temporary bag. Card details, passwords and personal information are not included in that bag backup.
